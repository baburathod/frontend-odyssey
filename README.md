📌 About the Project
"The Life of a Developer" is a humorous, scroll-driven interactive storytelling experience built for the Frontend Odyssey Hackathon at IIT Patna.
It takes the viewer through 6 chapters of a developer's life — from typing their first Hello World to surviving production outages at 3AM. Every scroll reveals a new chapter, animation, and relatable moment from the developer journey.
Theme chosen: The Life of a Developer — A humorous interactive journey (Learning HTML, debugging, deadlines, coffee addiction)
